# Editorial charter — DigiKat annual report

Status: fixed for **Izvještaj 0**; public-edition ratification remains a PI decision.

## Name

Recommended product name:

> **DigiKat — Katoličke teme u digitalnom prostoru: godišnji pregled evidentiranoga korpusa**

The stable product name is “DigiKat — Katoličke teme u digitalnom prostoru”. A geographic subtitle may be
added only after the source frame, language, and geography are operationally validated; the current corpus
includes Croatian and regional, including Bosnian, content.

Alternatives considered:

1. **Katoličke teme u hrvatskom digitalnom prostoru: godišnji pregled.** Precise locally, but Croatia is baked
   into the permanent title.
2. **DigiKat godišnji izvještaj o katoličkim medijima.** Short, but too narrow: the corpus includes secular
   coverage of Catholic themes, not only Catholic media.
3. **DigiKat Digital Catholic Media Report — Croatia.** Scalable internationally, but the English-first title is
   poorly matched to the primary Croatian audience.

## Cadence

Publish one annual flagship and generate one light mid-year data brief from the same pipeline.

- The flagship is the governed product: stable indicator definitions, full four-chapter spine, special chapter,
  methodology, and annex.
- The mid-year brief is a build target, not a second manuscript. It reuses a strict subset of the same generated
  indicators and contains no rotating chapter or actor annex.
- If the mid-year build requires separate analysis or prose maintenance, it is skipped for that cycle.

## Publication rule and data cutoff

- The publication date is World Communications Day in the Catholic calendar. The exact date is confirmed for
  each edition before the production calendar is issued.
- The reporting period is the preceding calendar year, ending on 31 December.
- January is reserved for closing ingestion, de-duplication, aggregate preview, and coverage reconciliation.
- February and March are for report aggregates, analysis, special-chapter integration, and drafting.
- April is for disclosure, numeric, domain, copy, and accessibility review.
- The final copy is frozen before a short embargo pre-brief. Covered actors receive no editorial veto or input.

Izvještaj 0 is an internal structural pilot using calendar 2025 and is not tied to a public release date.

## Audience and register

Audience order:

1. HBK Committee for Social Communications;
2. HKM leadership and editors of Catholic and secular outlets;
3. scholars, funders, and other observatory partners.

The report begins with findings and explains methods in plain Croatian boxes at the point of use. Technical
detail moves to methodology or annexes. The target public edition is approximately 25–30 pages. The main report
is Croatian; only the executive summary is translated into English.

The voice is measured computational social science for an informed non-specialist. It distinguishes observation
from interpretation, avoids advocacy and ranking theatre, and states uncertainty where it changes the reading.

## Permanent anatomy

1. **Sažetak** — approximately ten findings, each with one generated number; written last and designed first.
2. **Executive summary** — English translation of the summary only, using identical scalar keys.
3. **Metodologija** — corpus, reporting year, inclusion-rule pointer, stream seam, platform coverage, label
   coverage, suppression, and open-data links.
4. **Mapa ekosustava** — volume, platform composition, actors, typology, and engagement benchmarks.
5. **Tematske struje** — the frozen thematic dictionary, profile, and validated movers.
6. **Atmosfera diskursa** — tone and conflict measures, overall and for leading themes.
7. **Fokus na događaje** — pre-specified attention peaks and reviewed event labels.
8. **Rotating special chapter** — one completed DigiKat thematic study, clearly separated from recurring
   indicators.
9. **Annex: institutional mirror profiles** — one page per approved institutional outlet.
10. **Declarations and technical notes.**

## Independence and licensing

- Editor: Luka Šikić, Hrvatsko katoličko sveučilište.
- Funding and institutional support are disclosed in every edition.
- Covered actors have no role in indicator selection, analysis, interpretation, or editorial approval.
- An embargo pre-brief is informational and cannot change results except by identifying a verifiable factual
  error.
- Shareable report text and reviewed aggregates are released under CC BY 4.0 unless a source-specific right
  requires a narrower treatment.
- Named profiles are limited to institutional media outlets. Individual accounts are never profiled.

## Change control

Each indicator has a stable ID and definition version. A definition may change only when the change is recorded
in `INDICATORS.md`, its effect on the back series is tested, and the edition either regenerates the comparable
history or prints a visible series break. Editorial convenience is not a reason to redefine a measure.

## Pilot-specific editorial decisions

- Reporting year: 2025.
- Special chapter: provisional extract from *Socijalni nauk Crkve u javnoj raspravi o gospodarstvu*.
- Current non-stream-aware aggregates support a 2025 snapshot, not a 2024–2025 trend claim.
- Unratified confessional/secular labels are shown only as indicative and always with classified coverage.
- Missing annual measures appear as gap panels rather than simulated findings.

## Edition 2 (reporting year 2024) — editorial decisions, PI, 2026-08-10

- **Framing.** Published as a full "Godišnji pregled 2024.", with the permanent anatomy intact. The
  interruption is disclosed in the summary, the methods box, the year line and the gap panels rather
  than used to retitle the product as a partial-year retrospective.
- **Special chapter.** *Inflacija i crkveni govor*, from `studies/inflation-salience`. The chapter
  rotates by charter rule; reusing edition 1's would make the rotation decorative.
- **No comparison to 2023.** The collection stream begins during 2024, so the year has no
  instrument-comparable predecessor. The edition publishes a within-year, within-stream quarter step
  and prints the missing year-over-year measure as a gap panel.
- **Back-editions.** A back-edition is the same governed chain at a different reporting year, never a
  copy of it. Prose may differ per edition; measures and chart forms may not.
