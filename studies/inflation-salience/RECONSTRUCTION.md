# Pipeline reconstruction — outcome and residuals

## Official-corpus refresh — 11 August 2026

The paper was re-run on `data/digikat_corpus.rds` (413,985 rows; manifest generated 10 August
2026). The corpus preserves the accumulator source row in `dk_master_row`, which makes it
possible to migrate the earlier annotations without URL matching or dependence on row order.

The refreshed selector yields 1,486 cost-of-living posts and 761 proximity candidates. Earlier
three-run annotations cover 729 candidates; a separate single-coder addendum covers 32. The
resulting domestic measured set contains 475 posts: 465 retained prior decisions and 10
addendum decisions. The original 520-post outputs remain recoverable from version control, but
the active EMIP v2 manuscript and generated assets now report the official-corpus analysis.

The main timing result survives: direct own-position speech peaks in October 2022 and repricing
coverage in June 2024. The early headline-HICP correlation weakens to 0.48. This shift is treated
as a substantive finding, not tuned away. Scripts 01–17, the nine table fragments, both figures,
the 154 manuscript scalars and the replication package were rebuilt end to end.

## What was recovered exactly, and why it matters most

**The join key.** `PAPER_v1` used a column called `rid` without saying what it was, and the execution
brief concluded it had been generated by the missing script and could not be reconstructed, so the
coded core would have to be joined back to the master on `URL`. That is not the case. `rid` is the
1-based row index of the master data frame: at every one of the 1 450 pooled row numbers, both the URL
and the date in the master agree with the pool index. `03_finalize_coded.R` asserts this and stops if
it ever fails.

Two consequences. Six pooled items share a URL with another row, so a URL join would have been
ambiguous for them. And the collection stream is now exact for all 520 core posts rather than only for
the 88 dated 2024, which is what the stream conditioning the paper depends on actually needs.

**The core definition.** The measured core is `c_infl == 1 & c_link == 1 & c_foreign == 0`. Taking
linkage and domesticity alone yields 532, because thirteen items the annotators judged not to be about
inflation at all carry a linkage label. The three-condition rule reproduces the 520-row file exactly,
rid for rid.

## Residual 1 — the tagging lexicon is close but not identical

`PAPER_v1` §3 lists the anchored vocabulary (`inflacij*`, `poskup*`, price-rise phrases, cost of
living, purchasing power, with the bare word `cijena` excluded) and `VALIDATION.md` §v3 describes the
metaphor guard, but neither states the regular expressions. The rebuilt lexicon in `_lib.R` was fitted
against the 39 monthly counts in `h1_attention_hicp_series.csv` rather than against the single total,
which is a far tighter constraint, and it lands 22 posts above the June figure out of roughly eight
thousand.

The metaphor guard is the larger part of the gap. It removes 71 posts where the June run removed 86,
and the list of abstract nouns it keys on ("inflation of words", "of values", "of superlatives") is
documented by example rather than in full, so the last fifteen are not recoverable by reading.

The monthly series is the quantity the paper actually uses, and there the agreement is strong: the
denominators are identical in all 39 months, the inflation counts differ by 1,74 posts a month on
average against a mean of about 63, and the two attention shares correlate at 0,9973.

## Residual 2 — the proximity filter is broader than the original

The rebuilt filter generates 1 704 candidates and contains 1 370 of the 1 450 that were coded, so it
recovers 94,5% of the June pool while adding 334 items of its own. The specification that survives
gives the window (±220 characters) and the homonym problems that were fixed (`Crveni križ`,
`trapist` cheese, `Papa-test`, `posveć`, `gospodarstvo` against `Gospa`) but not the tightened
religious pattern list, and the religious lexicon has many roots whose secular readings are common —
`gospodin` for "mister", `misao` for "thought", `sestra` for a family sister. Which of these the June
run suppressed is not documented.

The window mechanics are provably unrecoverable as well. The 520 surviving `window` excerpts are not
substrings of the master text for most rows and vary in length from 229 to 566 characters, so the
original applied a text normalization and a snapping rule that no surviving file describes.

**This does not affect any published quantity.** The 1 450 items that were coded are fixed and on
disk; the filter that selected them is upstream of the annotation and is not itself reported. What the
rebuild bounds is how stable that selection is under an independent reimplementation, which is a
robustness statement worth making in the paper rather than a defect in it.

## A defect found and corrected

Six posts carry the date **31 December 2022** in the master and in the June core file itself, but that
file's `year` column assigns them to 2023. `PAPER_v1` Table 4 therefore reads 2022 → 201 and
2023 → 100. The date is unambiguous and the correct split is **2022 → 207, 2023 → 94**. All other year
totals are unaffected. The scripts derive the year from the date and the corrected figures are what
the rewrite uses.

This slightly sharpens the paper's own result: the 2022 peak is larger and the 2023 decline steeper
than published.

## Residual 3 — the recoding is not cross-family

`EMIP_EXECUTION.md` Step 4 asks for the validation slice to be recoded by a model from a
**different family** than the three that produced the June labels, on the reasoning that
agreement across independent families is a materially stronger claim than agreement among
runs of one family. That bar is not met.

The recoding was done by a different model, blind to the existing labels and working from
the same written protocol, and it is a genuine independent run rather than a fourth pass
by the same annotator. But it belongs to the same model family as the original annotators,
which is the only family reachable from this machine. The manuscript states this in
Section 3.5 and does not describe the check as cross-family anywhere.

Two things would close it, and both are decisions rather than work. An API key for a model
from another vendor would allow the recoding to run locally under the same conditions.
Alternatively the excerpts could be sent to a hosted service, but that means transmitting
scraped post text to a third party, which the study's own disclosure rule forbids and
which is the PI's call rather than a technical detail. The sheet
(`output/private/reannotation_sheet.csv`, 173 items, no labels) and the scoring script are
already in place, so whichever route is taken the work is one run.

## What would close the residuals

Re-coding the 334 candidates the rebuilt filter adds and the 80 it misses would replace the June pool
with a fully reproducible one and retire both residuals at once. That changes the coded core and
therefore every validated number downstream, so it is a deliberate decision for the PI and not
something to fold into a submission on this timetable. It is the obvious first task after submission.

## How to re-run

```powershell
& 'C:\Program Files\R\R-4.6.0\bin\Rscript.exe' studies/inflation-salience/RUN_ALL.R
```

Thirteen scripts, about seven minutes on this machine. `--no-corpus` skips the five that read the
master, which is what a reader of the replication package does; `--no-network` uses the archived price
series instead of retrieving it. Scripts `01` and `03` print their own fixture reports.

The numbering differs slightly from `EMIP_EXECUTION.md`, which did not anticipate the Eurostat
retrieval or the recoding needing scripts of their own. The mapping is `00` price data,
`01`–`03` the rebuilt pipeline, `04` the sector profile (Step 2), `05` the instrument check (Step 3),
`06`–`07` the recoding (Step 4), `08`–`11` tables, sync, checks and typesetting (Steps 5 and 7),
`12` the replication package (Step 8).

For the economic reframe, add `--v2`: scripts `14`–`16` prepare the blind sheets, ingest the
three independent labels and construct the event process before the v2 table, checking, rendering
and replication stages. The verified full command ran 15 scripts in 7.4 minutes on 2026-08-05.
