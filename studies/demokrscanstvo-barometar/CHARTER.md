# Charter

Owner: Luka Šikić, Hrvatsko katoličko sveučilište. Product: descriptive media barometer and
edition-bound conference summary. Initial conference: 24 September 2026, “Izazovi i budućnost
demokršćanstva u Hrvatskoj i Europi”.

The population is a ratified fixed panel of Croatian online news outlets from DetermDB, not the
official DigiKat topic corpus. Confessional and political portals may participate if they satisfy
editorial and continuity criteria, with segment facets and sensitivity results. Institutional,
aggregator and non-news websites are excluded. Panel membership is set without topic numerators.

Rules classify coverage of ideas, not people or publishers. The v1 actor registry is limited to
organisations. No source article text, headline, quotation, article URL, personal metadata or
per-outlet topic rate is published. Editorial independence excludes promotional and service copy.

Panel, definition, validation, first installation, full render and publication have separate PI
gates. A deadline does not replace a missing human validation result. The assistant may propose
aggregate-supported findings; the PI approves their wording. Human labels are reported as human,
assistant diagnostics as assistant; neither is silently substituted for the other.

The PI withdrew the special D8 AI passage-access procedure. The development/evaluation split and
methodological audit remain. The instruction excluding Andrea is respected in participation;
the source-reuse question is unresolved and cannot be answered by removing an attribution alone.
No contact with collaborators is authorized by this charter.

Weekly updates, when upstream data exist, update series and figures. Findings and PDF summaries
remain pinned to monthly editions. Every published numerical revision is recorded. Definition or
panel changes require new versions, full-history recomputation and fresh validation.
