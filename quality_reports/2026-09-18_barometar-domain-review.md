## Construct judgement

A1 measures explicit reference to a tradition or idea, including criticism and negation. A2 and unresolved A? are appropriately kept outside both scopes. B is an application of identifiable social teaching to a political question; C is a grounded argument whose concepts meet the declared threshold. D adds a diagnostic attribution to already included material. The separation prevents routine party or church coverage from becoming the principal indicator by definition.

The broad union is a composite of Christian-democratic and related Christian-social discourse, not a direct measure of support for Christian democracy or the political success of its parties. The opening and limitations already avoid those inferences; future titles and Nalazi must preserve both scope names. Church attribution identifies the source of an argument, not its theological correctness or the beliefs of the outlet.

The theological distinctions are defensible. The Compendium describes Catholic social doctrine as theological-moral teaching, distinguishes principles from domains of social life, and discusses migration in its chapter on work (§§297–298). Its discussion does not make all Catholic social teaching identical with a party programme. The six thematic groups are therefore an analytic adaptation; they are not six exhaustive or mutually exclusive doctrinal propositions. These checks used the official English text; the attempted official Croatian URL was unavailable, so this pass does not certify exact Croatian-edition chapter wording. [Vatican Compendium, §§68–73, 160–163, 297–298 and Part II](https://www.vatican.va/roman_curia/pontifical_councils/justpeace/documents/rc_pc_justpeace_doc_20060526_compendio-dott-soc_en.html).

The conference title, institution and 24 September 2026 date match the institution's official announcement. This verification does not establish a particular speaker's programme slot. [HKS event announcement](https://www.unicath.hr/event/znanstveno-strucni-skup-izazovi-i-buducnost-demokrscanstva-u-hrvatskoj-i-europi).

## Actor and sensitivity limitations

Documented actor intervals are evidence boundaries, not dates of ideological conversion. Excluded pending records remain inactive; aliases and generic church-role expressions do not imply complete entity recognition. HDZ BiH is foreign and distinct from the domestic HDZ record. The HDZ-only flag means “only according to the declared registry and role vocabulary,” not that no other real actor occurs in the article.

Removing church-attributed material changes the composition of the broad construct because attribution can itself supply C's Christian grounding. A reduction under that sensitivity cannot by itself show church dominance, bias, mediation or causation. Likewise, the HDZ variant is a robustness diagnostic, not an estimate of HDZ's ideological influence.

## Skeptical-referee assessment

The strongest alternative explanations for a future change are the collection break, outlet/output composition, repeated or omitted text, event-driven attention, different detectability of explicit names and paraphrases, and lexical attribution errors. The draft acknowledges the fixed panel, missingness, bridge, language limits, separate development/evaluation and the restricted recall-probe frame. Human precision and agreement remain prerequisites, with explicit failure branches.

The method-only status is consistent throughout: there are no measured route precision values, trend statements, popularity claims or empirical Nalazi in the reviewed draft. The numerical validation thresholds are correctly presented as requirements, not achieved performance. A fixed panel does not become a census of Croatian media, and article counts do not measure audience exposure or engagement.

This review supports the method-only presentation subject to the wording clarifications above. It does not approve an empirical release, human coding, scientific findings, source licensing or L1 verification.
