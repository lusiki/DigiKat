The author requested a finished public resource, removal of provisional and
author-facing copy, and a substantive NLP analysis of the collected material.

1. Rewrite the Croatian page around findings, themes, platforms and time.
   Keep concise definitions and chart-reading information in the method section.
2. Build a separate reproducible thematic release from the 2,306 included
   records. Reconstruct qualifying contexts, lemmatize Croatian locally, fit
   TF–IDF/NMF topics on distinct contexts, and inspect topic terms and examples.
   Publish only aggregates, editorial topic descriptions and method metadata.
3. Add an accessible thematic explorer with platform and year filters, topic
   profiles and counts; retain a useful static view without JavaScript.
4. Reconcile the new counts against the existing release, test calculations,
   render the page, and check desktop/mobile layout, keyboard and accessibility.

The existing inclusion definition, source database, official DigiKat corpus and
processed site aggregates are outside the mutation scope. Historical validation
metadata remains factual; the public page will not imply completed validation.
No deployment or commit is requested in this turn.
