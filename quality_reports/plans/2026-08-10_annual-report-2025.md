# Plan — DigiKat godišnji izvještaj 2025 (HTML + PDF)

Date: 2026-08-10 · Owner: PI (Luka Šikić) · Author of plan: Claude Code session

## Goal

Produce the 2025 annual report as a finished, reader-facing product in **HTML and PDF**, written and
designed under `.claude/rules/annual-report-writing.md`, structured under `studies/annual-report/CHARTER.md`,
and measured under `INDICATORS.md`. Every number generated; no hand-typed scalars.

## What changed since Izvještaj 0 (the reason this is a rebuild, not an edit)

Izvještaj 0 was computed from `data/processed/` at the **2026-07-28 vintage**, which was built from the
**accumulator** (`data/merged_comprehensive.rds`, 710 307 rows). On 2026-08-10 the project cut the
**official corpus** (`data/digikat_corpus.rds`, 413 985 rows) under one inclusion rule for both eras and
**rebuilt `data/processed/` from it** (processed manifest input sha256 matches the corpus manifest).

Consequence: **every headline number in Izvještaj 0 is stale.** 2025 is 124 346 posts in the corpus, not
236 166 (that is the accumulator's 2025 row count). The report must be regenerated end to end.

Second consequence: `data/nlp/` and `data/page-ready/` are **still accumulator-derived** (sha256 of the
master in `data/nlp/manifest.json` is the accumulator's). Their samples therefore mix corpus and
non-corpus rows. Re-running udpipe over the corpus is hours of compute and is out of scope here.

## Decisions taken

1. **Corpus-restricted NLP layers.** The three NLP samples join to the corpus on `URL` with **zero**
   unmatched keys. Membership rate in the 2025 slice is 52,9 % against the corpus's own 2025 keep rate of
   52,7 % — the join reproduces the rule, so filtering the existing samples to corpus members yields an
   (approximately 5 %/3 %/2 %) stratified random sample **of the corpus**. Theme, tone and event layers are
   recomputed on the filtered documents using the project's own `R/lib/page_summaries.R` scoring
   (`digikat_enrich_themes`, `digikat_document_atmosphere`) and `R/lib/thematic_dictionaries.R`. Tokens are
   reused; udpipe is not re-run.
2. **Report-local stream-aware annual aggregates**, read once from the corpus read-only. This is
   `PIPELINE.md` step 4 of the public runbook and closes gap #1. It gives: platform × month × stream,
   an actor × platform × year panel for 2025, and one *stream-conditioned* comparison
   (H2 2024 vs H2 2025, both entirely inside the post-2024 collection stream). No comparison crosses the
   2024 instrument seam.
3. **Full sixteen-category thematic table** computed for 2025 under one denominator (closes gap "Complete
   annual sixteen-theme table"), instead of the seven-of-sixteen guided object.
4. **Annual tone and conflict** for 2025 (score + conflict language index), with n and uncertainty per
   cell, instead of the all-period display diagnostic.
5. **Full-calendar event scoring**: 365 days materialised, zero-sample days are zeros, z-scores over the
   complete calendar.
6. **The composite headline index is NOT invented.** It is a measure, `INDICATORS.md` has no such
   indicator, and the charter forbids adding one outside change control. It appears as a gap panel.
   Same for "što smo rekli prošle godine" (edition 2 onward) and for change arrows against a previous
   edition (none exists).
7. **Event naming.** The dominant 2025 peak is the papal transition (Francis's death, 21 April 2025;
   funeral 26 April; Leo XIV elected 8 May 2025). The label is a public-calendar fact cross-checked
   against the data, not an inference from the peak — stated as such, with the naming protocol named.
8. **Status.** The report is produced as the real product, with the charter's independence and licensing
   block, and one plain line in the colophon recording that publication requires PI sign-off. No
   red "internal pilot" banner over the findings.

## Architecture (files)

| File | Role |
|---|---|

| `01_report_aggregates.R` | corpus read-only → all report-local CSVs (volume, stream, actors, labels) |

| `typeset/report.css`, `typeset/typst-template.typ`, `typeset/typst-show.typ` | the two designs |

Old `REPORT_0_TEMPLATE.qmd` and the 00–05 pilot chain are replaced in place; the study is untracked, so
no git history is disturbed.

## Guard rails

- Writes only below `studies/annual-report/output/`. Never touches `data/**`, `R/03_aggregate.R`,
  `_quarto.yml`, `docs/`. Render happens in a temp dir outside the repo; `docs/` is fingerprinted
  before and after.
- The corpus and the accumulator are opened **read-only**; neither is rewritten.
- Named actors: institutional outlets only, `publish = yes` in the PI sidecar, ≥ 5 posts. Individuals
  never named. Row-level text and URLs never leave `output/private/`.
- Croatian decimal comma, thin-space/point thousands, diacritics verified end to end.

## Definition of done

The eight boxes in `.claude/rules/annual-report-writing.md`, minus the per-edition derivatives (deck,
infographic) and the embargo pre-brief, which are PI-side steps. The 3-number press release is produced.
