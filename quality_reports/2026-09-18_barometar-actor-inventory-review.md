# Barometar: actor inventory and primary-source review

Date: 2026-09-18. Independent pre-G2 development review; no source-article text or empirical indicators were used here. The audited dictionary snapshot is `1.0.0+745f6b2b1f5e`.

## Recommended inventory amendment

Every active actor matcher must map to a registry record with complete aliases, organisation role, country, dated validity, status and exact evidence references. A group of foreign-party codes is not a substitute for one record per organisation. Alias coverage and ideological classification are separate: a full party name may support A2, while affiliation alone never supplies D. HDZ and HDZ BiH must remain separate, with the foreign alias taking precedence over a nested domestic abbreviation.

The snapshot contains five actor matcher entries but only two registry records, HDZ and HBK. Both records lack the brief's explicit aliases field. Some church-role strings also identify organisations outside the actor matcher group. They need the same registry crosswalk when used as named organisation speakers.

Enumerate all required candidate entities now. Retain every unverified entity as `status: excluded_pending_evidence`, with safe full-name aliases only, explicit proposed role, missing-evidence reason and no active rule or D facet. A proposed status is not proof of a role or historical interval. Compilation should reject any active alias whose organisation, status or relevant date cannot be resolved.
